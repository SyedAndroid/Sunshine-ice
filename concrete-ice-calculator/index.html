How the calculation works

The calculator uses the heat balance method for fresh concrete temperature given in ACI 305R, the American Concrete Institute guide to hot weather concreting. The heat held in the cement, aggregates and water is balanced against the heat the ice absorbs as it melts.

Ice replaces an equal weight of mixing water, so the water content of your mix stays the same. Solving the heat balance for the ice quantity gives:

Ice = [ 0.22 × (TaWa + TcWc) + TwWw − T × (0.22 × (Wa + Wc) + Ww) ] ÷ (79.6 + Tw)

  • Wa, Wc, Ww are the weights of aggregates, cement and mixing water in kg per cubic metre.
  • Ta, Tc, Tw are their temperatures in °C, and T is the target placing temperature.
  • 0.22 is the specific heat of cement and aggregates compared with water.
  • 79.6 is the heat one kg of ice absorbs as it melts, in kcal.

A worked example

Take a 500 cubic metre pour with 400 kg cement, 1,850 kg aggregates and 160 kg water per cubic metre. The cement is at 50°C, the aggregates at 32°C and the water at 28°C. Without ice this concrete would be about 33.4°C.

To place it at 25°C, each cubic metre needs about 51 kg of ice, which is 32% of the mixing water. The whole pour needs about 25.7 tonnes, or 234 slabs of 110 kg.

What the estimate leaves out

  • Melt before batching. Some ice melts during transport and handling. Add your own allowance when you order.
  • Moisture in the aggregates. Water carried by wet aggregates counts as mixing water but cannot be replaced with ice.
  • Heat gained after mixing. Mixing energy, transit time and hot weather warm the concrete on the way to the pour.
  • Colder ice. The method treats ice as 0°C. Our blocks are hardened to a −8°C core, which gives a small extra margin.

Common questions

Ice dosing for concrete, answered

How much ice is needed to cool concrete by 1°C?

For a typical mix of 400 kg cement, 1,850 kg aggregates and 160 kg water per cubic metre, about 6 kg of ice per cubic metre lowers the concrete temperature by 1°C. The exact figure depends on your mix and the temperature of the mixing water, so use the calculator with your own numbers.

How does the concrete ice calculator work?

It uses the heat balance method for fresh concrete temperature given in ACI 305R, the American Concrete Institute guide to hot weather concreting. The heat in the cement, aggregates and water is balanced against the heat the ice absorbs as it melts. Ice replaces an equal weight of mixing water, so the water content of the mix does not change.

How much of the mixing water can be replaced with ice?

Ice is usually limited to about 75% of the mixing water, because some water is needed to batch and mix the concrete properly. The calculator warns you when a target temperature needs more than that. Your mix design and batching plant set the actual limit.

Is the result the final ice dose for my pour?

No. It is an estimate for planning and ordering. Your QC engineer or batching plant sets the final dose from the approved mix design and trial results. Allow extra for ice that melts during transport and handling.
