Crushed from solid slabs, at dispatch

Our crushed ice starts as the same 110 kg slabs we supply to construction sites. Each slab is frozen slowly in chilled brine tanks and hardened until the core reaches −8°C.

We crush the slabs at loading, not hours earlier, and deliver in insulated loads. Ice that starts colder and is crushed later arrives with less melt.

What customers use it for

  • Fisheries and fish traders. Crushed ice packed straight over the catch to keep it fresh from landing to market.
  • Fruit and vegetable processing. Holding produce cold through washing, sorting, packing and transport.
  • Food processing lines. Lines that need ice packed over the product.
  • Events and catering. Bulk ice for weddings, exhibitions and catering, delivered ahead of your setup window.
  • Concrete batching plants. Crushed ice for plants that dose it into the mix. See ice for concrete cooling.
