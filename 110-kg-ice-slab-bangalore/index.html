How the blocks are made

  • Moulds filled. Water is filled into steel moulds sized for full slabs and half blocks, then lowered into the freezing tanks.
  • Brine-tank freezing. Blocks freeze slowly in chilled brine. Slow freezing gives a dense, clear block with fewer air pockets and a slower melt.
  • Hardening. Blocks are held until the core reaches −8°C, so they arrive solid even after a long summer run.
  • Weigh and dispatch. Blocks are weighed and moved in covered, insulated vehicles.
