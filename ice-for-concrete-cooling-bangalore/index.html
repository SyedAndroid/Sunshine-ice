Why concrete needs ice

Cement gives off heat as it hydrates. On a large pour in a Bengaluru summer, that heat adds to warm aggregates and warm mixing water, and the concrete can go in hotter than the project specification allows. Hot concrete loses workability faster and is more likely to crack as it cools.

Replacing part of the mixing water with ice is one of the most direct ways to bring the temperature down. Ice absorbs far more heat as it melts than chilled water can, and the melt water becomes part of the mix water.
